UNDERSTANDING THE SCOPE

What this dataset does—and does not—tell us.

The model predicts a crop category using the seven features included in the selected dataset. The dataset does not include every real-world farming factor, such as location, soil type, season, irrigation, market conditions or expected yield. CropWise is an educational decision-support prototype, not a guarantee of agricultural suitability.
